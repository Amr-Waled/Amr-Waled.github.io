# Digital Product Launch & Distribution

Even the best digital product fails if nobody knows it exists. This skill governs high-impact distribution.

---

## 1. The Pre-Launch Waitlist Engine (2-4 Weeks Before)

- **The Teaser Landing Page**: Clear 1-sentence value proposition + animated GIF/video mockup of the core feature.
- **Gamified Viral Waitlist**:
  - "You are #142 on the waitlist. Refer 3 friends to get Instant Beta Access + 3 Months Free Pro."
- **Build In Public (Twitter/X & LinkedIn)**:
  - Share the raw numbers, UI mockups, tech choices, and behind-the-scenes struggles.
  - Tag influential creators and tech founders in your niche.

---

## 2. The Product Hunt Launch Playbook

1. **Timing**: Launch on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday at 12:01 AM PST (Pacific Time).
2. **Visual Assets**:
   - Animated GIF Logo (240x240px) to stand out in the feed.
   - Gallery images (1270x760px) showing real UI workflows with bold, readable benefit headlines.
   - 60-second Loom/Screen demo video.
3. **First Comment (Maker Comment)**:
   - Share the personal origin story: Why did you build this? What problem broke your heart?
   - Offer an exclusive discount code: "PH50 for 50% off lifetime".
4. **Community Mobilization**:
   - Notify your waitlist, newsletter, Discord/Slack communities, and LinkedIn connections throughout the 24-hour cycle.

---

## 3. Alternative Distribution Channels

- **Micro-Marketplaces**:
  - **AppSumo**: Great for instant cash flow (Lifetime Deals - LTDs) and 5,000+ early testers.
  - **Lemon Squeezy & Gumroad**: Great for digital assets, templates, and boilerplates.
  - **TheresAnAIForThat & Futurepedia**: Essential directories for AI tools.
- **Reddit & Indie Hackers**:
  - Never post blatant ads. Post high-value case studies: "How I built a tool that solved [problem] in 14 days (code & lessons learned)".
