# Social Media Design, Carousels & Visual Branding Skill

A complete visual design guide for engineers, tech founders, and professionals building an authoritative personal brand on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

---

## 1. High-Converting Carousel Blueprint (LinkedIn & Instagram)

Carousels have the highest average save rate and engagement rate across LinkedIn (as PDF documents) and Instagram (as multi-slide 4:5 posts).

### The 5-Part Slide Anatomy:
1. **Slide 1: The Cover (The Hook)**
   - **Visual Goal**: Stop the thumb scroll in 0.5 seconds.
   - **Headline**: Big, bold text (max 8-10 words). Use a contrasting highlight word.
   - **Visual Element**: High-quality UI mock, code snippet, diagram preview, or personal portrait.
   - **Swipe Indicator**: Subtle arrow or "Swipe →" micro-cue.
2. **Slide 2: The Context & Problem**
   - Agitate the pain point or myth (e.g., "Most people think you need 6 months of coding to build a CRM. Here is what actually happened...").
3. **Slides 3-7: The Core Value / Meat**
   - **Rule**: One core idea per slide.
   - Large headers + 2-3 bullet points or visual diagrams per slide.
   - Ample white space; avoid walls of text.
4. **Slide 8-9: The Synthesis / Cheatsheet**
   - A single-slide summary diagram or checklist consolidating the lesson. This is what drives **Saves** and **Reposts**.
5. **Slide 10: The CTA (Conversion Slide)**
   - Clear profile picture, name, headline, and specific call to action:
     - "Follow @AmrWaled for more AI-native building breakdowns."
     - "Drop a comment with [Keyword] to get the architecture template."

## 2. Personal Brand Visual Identity & Design Tokens

### Aspect Ratios by Platform:
- **LinkedIn Carousels**: 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 (1080x1350) exported as high-resolution PDF.
- **Instagram Carousels & Posts**: 4:5 portrait (1080x1350) for maximum screen real estate.
- **LinkedIn Profile Banner**: 1584 x 396 px (Keep text and key elements in the right/center to avoid mobile avatar overlap).
- **Facebook Cover**: 820 x 312 px (Desktop) / 640 x 360 px (Mobile safe area).
- **YouTube Thumbnail**: 16:9 (1280x720) with high-contrast text and facial reaction or striking product screenshot.

### Typography Hierarchy:
- **Arabic Modern Tech**:
  - Primary / Headings: **Cairo Black / Bold** or **Almarai ExtraBold**.
  - Body Text: **Almarai Regular** or **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**.
- **English Tech Pairings**:
  - Headings: **Montserrat Bold**, **Plus Jakarta Sans**, or **Inter Display**.
  - Code / Tech Elements: **JetBrains Mono** or **Fira Code**.

### Color Palette Framework for Tech & Business:
- **Dark Mode Aesthetic (High Tech & Engineering)**:
  - Deep Navy / Slate Background (`#0F172A` or `#0B0F19`)
  - Crisp White Typography (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Accent Color (Primary Hook): Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`) or Emerald / Cyber Green (`#10B981`) or Vibrant Amber (`#F59E0B`)
- **Clean Light Aesthetic (Business & Executive)**:
  - Clean Paper White / Soft Gray (`#FFFFFF` / `#F1F5F9`)
  - Deep Charcoal Text (`#0F172A`)
  - Accent: Royal Cobalt Blue (`#1D4ED8`) or Violet (`#6366F1`)

## 3. Profile Banner Formulas (LinkedIn & Facebook)

### The 3-Zone Banner Layout:
1. **Left Zone**: Kept clean or minimal gradient (where the profile picture sits on desktop and mobile).
2. **Center/Right Zone (The Value Prop)**:
   - Big Line: "Architecting SaaS & Digital Systems at 10x Speed with AI"
   - Subline: "Vibe Coder | Marketing & Growth | Ex-President Enactus Tanta"
3. **Proof / CTA Strip**:
   - Logos/Mentions of shipped projects (CRM, Platforms, E-commerce).
   - "Follow for daily insights on AI building, growth, and SaaS."
