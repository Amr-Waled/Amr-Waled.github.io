# Product-Led Growth (PLG) & In-App Virality

Product-Led Growth is an end-to-end go-to-market strategy where the product itself drives acquisition, activation, conversion, and expansion.

---

## 1. The Core Pillars of PLG

1. **Zero-Friction Access**:
   - Eliminate unnecessary steps before showing value (e.g. allow users to test a core feature without signing up, or 1-click Google Auth).
2. **Time to First Value (TTFV)**:
   - The user must experience the "Aha! Moment" within 60 to 90 seconds of first entering the application.
3. **Inherent Virality & Network Effects**:
   - Every active user should naturally expose the product to other potential users through artifacts, shared links, invites, or "Powered by [Product]" badges.

---

## 2. Onboarding & Activation Engineering

- **Empty States That Teach**: Replace blank screens with pre-filled templates, dummy data, or interactive guided actions.
- **Progressive Profiling**: Never ask 10 onboarding questions up front. Ask 1-2 questions that customize their immediate workspace.
- **Checklist Psychology**: A 3-4 item checklist with item #1 already checked off (e.g., "[x] Account created", "[ ] Generate first output", "[ ] Share with a teammate").
- **Contextual Empty-State CTAs**: Big, welcoming button in the center: "Create your first project in 30 seconds".

---

## 3. The Freemium vs. Free Trial Architecture

| Model | When to Use | Conversion Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Freemium** | Low marginal cost per user, large Total Addressable Market (TAM), strong network effects. | Feature gating (Pro features), Usage limits (e.g., 3 exports/mo free, unlimited on Pro). |
| **Reverse Trial** | Premium features shown for 14 days without credit card required. | Once trial expires, app downgrades to basic tier unless they add a card. |
| **Usage-Based Credits** | AI tools, compute-intensive platforms, API services. | Free bundle (e.g., 50 free credits), pay-as-you-go top-ups or monthly refills. |

---

## 4. Viral Loops & Referral Mechanics

- **Collaborative Artifacts**: Exported PDFs, shared public dashboards, or generated images contain a subtle watermark/link: "Made with [Product Name]".
- **Double-Sided Referrals**: "Give $10 / 100 Credits, Get $10 / 100 Credits".
- **Team Seat Expansion**: When a user wants to share a project, prompt: "Invite your colleague with 1 click".
- **Milestone Celebrations**: When a user reaches a milestone (e.g., 100 sales, 1st report created), show a confetti modal with a 1-click "Share to X/LinkedIn" button.
